Questions

How do I add a letter?

Tap “Scan a letter” at the bottom of the screen. Scan it with the camera, choose photos, or import a PDF. No letter at hand? Try one of the fictional samples.

How do I import from Files, Preview or my scanner app?

In the other app, tap Share and choose LifeAdmin. If you don't see it, scroll the app row to the end, tap More and turn LifeAdmin on. Then open LifeAdmin: the letter opens for review.

Why does a deadline say “Needs a start date”?

Some letters give a period, like “within one month of delivery”, instead of a date. LifeAdmin asks when the letter arrived and shows the result as an estimate, because the legal start date can differ from the day it arrived. If it matters, confirm it with the institution.

How do I check where a fact comes from?

Tap the quoted line under any date, task or document. The original page opens with that line highlighted.

Why can't I type my own questions in the assistant?

Free-form questions need Apple Intelligence, which runs on supported iPhones and iPads with it turned on in Settings. The ready-made questions work on every device.

My data doesn't appear on my other device.

Check that both devices are signed in to the same iCloud account, that iCloud Drive is on, and that Profile → Sync with iCloud is on in LifeAdmin. Changes to that switch apply the next time you open the app. The first sync can take a few minutes.

Does LifeAdmin send my letters anywhere?

No. Letters are read on your device and only sync through your own iCloud. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Profile → Delete all my data. With iCloud sync on, this also removes it from your iCloud and your other devices.

Is LifeAdmin legal advice?

No. It explains paperwork and helps you organize it. For decisions with legal or financial consequences, ask the institution or a qualified advisor.
