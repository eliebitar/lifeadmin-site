LifeAdmin Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

In short: LifeAdmin doesn't collect any data. The developer has no server and can't see your letters, cases or anything else you store in the app. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.

Where your data is stored

Everything you add (scanned pages, recognized text, cases, tasks, deadlines, drafts and assistant conversations) is stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud and keep “Sync with iCloud” turned on in the app, it also syncs between your devices through your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). That data is in your iCloud account, under Apple's privacy policy; the developer has no access to it. If you turn on Advanced Data Protection for iCloud, Apple encrypts it end to end.

Without an iCloud account, or with sync turned off, your data stays only on your device (and in your device backups).

How your documents are processed

  • Text recognition uses Apple's Vision framework on your device.
  • Explanations and assistant answers use Apple Intelligence on your device, where available (Apple's on-device Foundation Models). On other devices, LifeAdmin uses its own rule-based reading on your device.
  • No document, text or question is sent to the developer or to any AI provider.

Network requests

LifeAdmin itself makes no network requests. The only network traffic comes from Apple's iCloud sync (if you use it). Links to official websites (for example Deutsche Rentenversicherung) open in your browser only when you tap them.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan letters. Asked the first time you scan.
  • Notifications: for deadline reminders, scheduled on your device. Asked when you first save a deadline.
  • Face ID or Touch ID: only if you turn on the app lock.
  • Photos: LifeAdmin uses the system photo picker, so it only sees the photos you choose.

All permissions are optional. The app works without them.

Importing from other apps

When you share a PDF or image to LifeAdmin from another app, it's copied into a private folder on your device that only LifeAdmin and its share extension can read, until you review it in the app.

Your control

  • Export: Profile → Export my data creates a folder with all your information and the original pages.
  • Delete: Profile → Delete all my data removes everything from your device and, when sync is on, from your iCloud and your other devices. Deleting the app also removes its data on that device.
  • You can also manage LifeAdmin's iCloud data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Children

LifeAdmin doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
